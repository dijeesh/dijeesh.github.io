Ctrl+Cloud — Speaking on Amazon EKS Auto Mode

aws kubernetes eks speaking community

It’s been a while since I delivered a talk, and this time I had the pleasure of organizing and speaking at the Ctrl+Cloud event, powered by CloudKeeper in association with AWS User Group Kochi and CNCG Kochi.

The session covered Amazon EKS Auto Mode — I hope it gave everyone a good starting point. Now go ahead and try it out yourself.
