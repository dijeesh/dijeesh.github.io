Speaking

Panel Discussion on Trusted AI Innovation for Nation Building — India AI Impact Summit 2026

Grateful to have been part of the panel discussion on “Trusted AI Innovation for Nation Building” conducted by Chinmaya Vishwavidyapeeth as part of the India AI Impact Summit 2026.

Alongside the panel discussion, an AWS Cloud Bootcamp session was conducted for the students, representing AWS User Group Kochi.

Exchanging perspectives with fellow speakers and panelists on building responsible, trustworthy, and impactful AI systems was equally enriching.

The new CVV campus is truly impressive. Sincere thanks to Anupama Jims and the organizing team for the invitation and the warm hospitality.

Ctrl+Cloud — Speaking on Amazon EKS Auto Mode

It’s been a while since I delivered a talk, and this time I had the pleasure of organizing and speaking at the Ctrl+Cloud event, powered by CloudKeeper in association with AWS User Group Kochi and CNCG Kochi.

The session covered Amazon EKS Auto Mode — I hope it gave everyone a good starting point. Now go ahead and try it out yourself.
