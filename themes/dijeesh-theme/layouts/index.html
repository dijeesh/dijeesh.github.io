About

I'm an engineering leader with 18+ years of experience building and scaling cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes platforms, and SRE organizations. I specialize in designing high-scale, cost-efficient systems across AWS, GCP, and Azure, with a strong focus on Kubernetes, containers, and AI/GPU workloads.

I currently lead Platform and R&D at Sedai, where I built a multi-cloud platform from the ground up and drive research in autonomous cloud optimization. My work spans Kubernetes cost optimization (including Karpenter tuning and node lifecycle management), GPU workload efficiency, and large-scale platform reliability — helping enterprise customers reduce cloud and AI infrastructure costs by 30–40%.

Previously, I led a 30–40 member cloud operations and SRE organization at Network Redux, where I founded and scaled the Managed AWS Services practice into a primary revenue driver. That experience shaped my approach to engineering leadership: combining deep technical rigor with strong systems thinking, team design, and operational discipline.

I'm recognized as an AWS Container Hero by Amazon Web Services for sustained contributions to the cloud-native ecosystem. I actively contribute to the community as an organizer of AWS User Group Kochi and CNCF Kochi, and through events like AWS Community Day and Kubernetes Community Day.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of cloud infrastructure and AI — where efficiency, reliability, and cost control are becoming core competitive advantages.
